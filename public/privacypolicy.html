RunLog — privacy (health data)

RunLog is a local-first running tracker. Your runs, profile, and settings stay on this device. There is no RunLog account and no RunLog cloud server.

Health Connect

If you choose Import from Health Connect, RunLog reads exercise sessions (and related distance, calories, and heart rate when available) that other apps — such as Samsung Health — have shared into Health Connect. That data is copied into RunLog’s on-device history so you can view it offline.

RunLog does not sell health data. Map tiles may be loaded from third-party map providers only when you view a map; that is separate from Health Connect.

You can revoke Health Connect access at any time in Android Settings → Health Connect.
